Japan Trip: Tokyo (Day 1) - Akihabara

2025.03.29

First day in Tokyo! Explored Akihabara, checked out anime shops, gaming arcades, and tried some local food.

Morning: Arrival in Akihabara

Started the day with a visit to the famous Electric Town. The streets were filled with colorful signs and the energy was incredible.

Afternoon: Anime and Gaming

Spent hours exploring various anime shops and gaming arcades. Found some amazing collectibles and tried out some rhythm games.

Evening: Local Food Experience

Ended the day with some delicious local cuisine at a small restaurant near the station. The ramen was absolutely amazing!
